## 1. The correction

v0.2 made one Readied Item Card consume an Attention Slot. That reduced ordinary
action capacity and caused 10.34% Main drought at four total positions. v0.2 then
added a Commitment guarantee to repair the pressure.

That was the wrong repair. It made the Brain/draw system more artificial because the
Item Card was competing with anatomy and technique for cognitive capacity.

v0.3 instead keeps:

- four ordinary Attention Slots for embodied Body and Technique Cards;
- one separate deliberately Readied Item Card lane;
- no automatic item replacement inside the encounter;
- imperfect weighted selection with no Attack/Defence/Main guarantee;
- a tightly bounded three-card Technique Package rather than a full active deck.

This preserves the owner's Item Card identity while borrowing document 39's strongest
inventory insight: inventory should not reduce the body's ordinary Attention capacity.

## 2. Optimized system summary

```text
current usable body
-> mandatory source-owned Body Core
-> choose three compatible Technique Imprints
-> Body Core + Technique Imprints form the Anatomical Deck
-> four weighted persistent Attention Slots
-> one separate deliberately Readied Item Card
-> Brain Parts bias or modify individual Attention Slots
-> Preparation/Main commitment and automatic cardless reflex
-> source loss invalidates dependent cards immediately
-> Decision Refresh refills Spent/Invalid Attention Slots
```

## 3. Ownership

| Layer | Owns | Does not own |
|---|---|---|
| Body | Physical capability, exact sources, mandatory Body Cards | Draw tendency, permanent Brain progression |
| Technique Package | Three player-selected compatible expressions of the current body | New anatomy, source substitution, a second full deck |
| Brain | Permanent roguelite slot configuration, paired buff/nerf tendencies | Physical capability, ordinary extra actions |
| Readied Item Card | One deliberately prepared inventory opportunity and its exact item state | Random draw, Attention capacity, automatic replacement |
| Attention | Four persistent Body/Technique opportunities | Inventory, reflexes, extra Preparation/Main actions |

## 4. Anatomical Deck

The diagnostic candidate contains:

- six mandatory Body Core cards, each from an exact usable source;
- exactly three selected Technique Cards;
- no Item Cards, automatic reflexes, state-required actions, or Brain Parts inside the
  deck.

The three-card Technique Package must contain:

- two Main cards;
- one Preparation card;
- at least two distinct exact sources;
- no duplicate card instance;
- only techniques compatible with the current body.

These constraints are structural guardrails, not final production counts. They stop
the player from unknowingly constructing a non-functional deck while leaving nine
distinct packages in the neutral six-Technique fixture. Four Techniques with three
Main cards were slightly more consistent, but allowed only three packages and weakened
build authorship. Two Techniques preserved nine packages but reduced Technique share
to roughly one quarter of visible action cards.

Technique acquisition and permanence remain deferred. v0.3 approves no second
permanent progression economy: the Brain remains the primary roguelite layer.

## 5. Attention and draw lifecycle

The diagnostic baseline uses four ordinary Attention Slots.

1. Rebuild the eligible Anatomical Deck from current source-valid Body and Technique
   instances.
2. Resolve slot filters and weights before seeded selection.
3. Select without replacement.
4. Unused valid cards persist in their slots.
5. Played cards become Spent.
6. Permanently invalid cards disappear immediately.
7. Spent and Invalid slots refill only at Decision Refresh.
8. Dormant cards remain visible with their exact blocking reason.
9. No composition or Commitment guarantee fabricates a perfect hand.

The candidate retains document 39's once-per-round, pre-lock, Blood-paid immediate
redraw grammar. The redraw is disabled when no alternative exists, previews the full
Blood consequence, excludes the rejected instance only for that redraw, and consumes
neither Preparation nor Main. Exact cost remains deferred.

## 6. Readied Item Card lane

The player deliberately chooses one owned Item Card before the encounter or at a
later-approved readiness boundary.

- It remains visibly a card.
- It does not enter random selection or occupy an Attention Slot.
- It keeps exact item identity, quantity, uses, expiry, required source, target, and
  timing.
- Playing it consumes its authored Preparation or Main opportunity.
- At most one voluntary inventory-origin action executes per round.
- Once used, lost, expired, or source-invalid, the lane stays empty.
- Another inventory item is never readied automatically.
- In-encounter re-readiness is deferred; if later allowed, it requires an explicit
  authored action and cannot become direct inventory access by another name.

This is the clean mix of both approaches: the owner's item-card presentation and
deliberate preparation survive, while the later non-competing inventory boundary
removes artificial hand pressure.

## 7. Brain Parts

Brain Parts remain rare permanent roguelite unlocks configured into individual
Attention Slots before a run and locked during that run.

Every ordinary Brain Part declares:

- one affected slot;
- one primary lever;
- one visible buff;
- one visible nerf, restriction, risk, or cost;
- exact source/timing/category conditions;
- a preview and evidence trace.

The primary families remain:

- **Access:** changes category/source/family tendency within legal candidates;
- **Execution:** modifies a card played from that slot while retaining every source,
  cost, range, commitment, and consequence rule;
- **Coordination:** reacts to named current body relationships without inventing a
  global instability meter;
- **Inventory exception:** rare and explicit; never places inventory in the ordinary
  draw.

An ordinary Brain Part modifies only one primary lever. Access plus execution on the
same ordinary Part is rejected because it double-dips into both availability and
power.

Execution Parts remain the highest-risk family. Equal numeric buff and nerf values do
not imply balance: the diagnostic +0.25/-0.25 Part was a pure +0.25 upgrade when its
nerf was ignored and neutral only when the entire penalty mattered. Production
Execution Parts require unavoidable or decision-changing downside evidence.

## 8. Action and causal rules

- Zero or one Preparation and zero or one Main may execute per actor per round.
- Attention capacity never creates another ordinary action.
- A Readied Item follows the same staged budget.
- Automatic reflex defence stays cardless and outside voluntary actions.
- Exact sources are revalidated before lock and execution.
- Two cards sharing one physical source cannot create an impossible combined plan.
- Source loss immediately removes dependent capability; replacement waits for
  Decision Refresh.
- Brain modifiers never restore or substitute a lost source.

## 9. Synthetic evidence

Across all nine legal three-Technique packages, 2,000 six-round sessions per package
and Brain condition produced:

| Condition | Main drought avg / worst | Post-loss avg / worst | Prep+Main coverage | Technique share | Attack share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| No Brain fixture | 0.92% / 1.52% | 1.67% / 2.85% | 97.74% | 33.31% | 20.88% |
| Access Brain fixture | 0.60% / 0.98% | 1.09% / 1.88% | 96.04% | 33.52% | 24.75% |

Six procedural personas then evaluated all nine packages across 250 seeds each.

- Main use ranged from 97.76% to 99.96% of rounds.
- At least two Main options appeared in 66.99% to 91.84% of rounds.
- At least two Main categories appeared in 53.10% to 77.77%.
- A Technique option appeared in 78.31% to 94.80%.
- Technique use ranged from 22.11% to 38.15% of selected actions.
- No single card exceeded 18.05% of selections.
- Bruiser used the Readied Item 0%; Survivor/Schemer/Adapter used it in 16.64-16.67%
  of rounds, the maximum for one non-refilling item across six rounds.

Twenty thousand generated state-machine configurations covering 100,041 rounds found
zero action-budget, item-timing, source-legality, shared-source-commitment, or
Commitment invariant failures.
All results reproduced deterministically.

This is structural evidence, not human evidence. The policy utilities, cards, Brain
weights, and encounter pressure are neutral diagnostics.

## 12. Remaining failure risks

- Preparation was used in 96-100% of rounds by five synthetic personas. Without
  representative costs, risks, or enemy pressure, Preparation may become routine
  administration rather than a decision.
- Survivor and Schemer policies remained relatively close because the neutral card
  grammar provides limited defensive Main expression. Automatic defence may make that
  correct, but only a representative encounter can decide it.
- Access Brain effects were visible but modest. Human players may not perceive the
  tendency change without excellent slot previews and history.
- The Readied Item decision worked structurally, but no synthetic score can establish
  whether readiness feels satisfying rather than artificial forgetting.
- Technique acquisition could compete with Brain progression and dilute body-as-build.
- Execution Brain Parts can dominate the body whenever their downside is avoidable.
- Persona policies prefer a Main whenever one is legal, so their Main-use percentage
  primarily measures availability rather than natural willingness to act.
- The Blood-paid redraw was not simulated; all candidate drought results are before
  redraw and no cost value is approved.
- The Access test applies one homogeneous weighting profile across all Attention
  selections as a dominance stress case. It is not final per-slot Brain content.

## 13. Verdict

v0.3 is the strongest optimized unique candidate produced so far. It preserves:

- the body as the source of physical capability;
- permanent paired Brain Parts as roguelite identity;
- imperfect weighted Attention;
- deliberately prepared Item Cards;
- bounded player deck authorship without a conventional full active deck.

It passes structural synthetic testing but is **not proven balanced, fun, readable,
fair, accessible, or replayable**. It must remain a proposal until a human diagnostic
tests whether Preparation, Technique authorship, Brain consequences, and the separate
Readied Item lane are understood and genuinely change decisions.
