## Condition safeguards

- Known Threat: read only the approved category clues: distant hoof impacts, a horned silhouette, a heavy cleaver, and evidence of torso/balance pressure. Never reveal numbers, roll thresholds, order, or that all dimensions appear.
- Unknown Threat: reveal no Warden identity, clues, or pressure category before table choice is locked.
- Read telegraphs verbatim from the participant cards. Roll openly using the assigned script/roll record; never reroll, soften, or silently correct a decision.
- Answer rules questions factually, record assistance verbatim or as a marked paraphrase, and do not give strategic advice.

## Provisional thresholds

After eight free-choice sessions, continue when at least 75% understand each telegraph by resolution/one neutral clarification, at least 75% understand Stand consumes the normal action, most distinguish Brace prevention from a failed Knockdown, and Guard is recognized in at least half of eligible opportunities. Continue table evidence when three options are naturally selected or unselected options remain defensible from explanations. Revise when the same rule confuses three sessions, facilitator help determines outcomes, Known is guessing, Unknown is blind punishment, or either script feels predetermined. These are decision aids, not statistical proof.

## Data integrity and privacy

- Record IDs only: no names, contact information, addresses, or unnecessary identifiers.
- Preserve raw records. Never rewrite statements to sound clearer; mark paraphrases as paraphrases.
- Record missing data as missing. Retain failed or confused sessions and facilitator deviations.
- Separate designer walkthrough, free-choice human, and directed-coverage evidence.
- Never combine Known/Unknown without showing both, infer a trend from one participant, claim significance, or infer fun from completion/ratings.
