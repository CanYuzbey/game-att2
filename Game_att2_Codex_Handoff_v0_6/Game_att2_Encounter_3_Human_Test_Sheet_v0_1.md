# Minotaur Warden Human Paper Test Sheet v0.1

Run time: 15-25 minutes. Give the participant either Known or Unknown information only; do not explain simulator internals or preferred choices.

## Before play

Explain: Blood is health/currency, the body has a grafted right arm, torso, and legs, and the table offers integrate, repair torso, strengthen legs, loan, or leave. Show only the chosen variant's information. Record starting body, Blood, table choice, and stated reason.

## After play questions

1. Why did you choose that table option?
2. What did you expect it to protect you from?
3. Which Warden action felt most dangerous?
4. Did Strengthened Legs feel useful?
5. Did torso repair feel useful?
6. Did integration feel useful?
7. Did conserving Blood feel valid?
8. Did the choice feel informed, guessed, or forced?
9. Would you choose the same option again?
10. Which rule was hardest to understand?
11. Did losing an action to Stand feel meaningful or merely annoying?
12. Did the Warden telegraphs feel readable before resolution?

Do not lead the participant toward any desired answer.
