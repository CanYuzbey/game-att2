## Your body and resources

Use the body state and Blood shown by the facilitator. Your torso can be vulnerable, your grafted right arm can use Guard Flesh, and your legs may be Braced after strengthening.

## Grafting Table

| Choice | What you know |
|---|---|
| Integrate arm | Make the graft a more secure part of your body. Costs 15 Blood. |
| Repair torso | Repair your damaged torso. Costs 18 Blood. |
| Strengthen legs | Strengthen your legs. Costs 12 Blood. |
| Table Loan | Gain 20 Blood now; owe 30 after the encounter. |
| Leave unchanged | Spend no Blood now. |

## Action timing

One Fast medical item may be used before your normal action. A normal action is one main choice. If Downed, Fast medical remains available, but your normal action must be Stand; you cannot also attack that turn.

## Conditions

- **Bleeding:** costs Blood at the start of a round. Clotting Cream can remove it.
- **Brace:** if present, automatically prevents the first successful Knockdown this encounter.
- **Downed:** Stand on your next normal action.
- **Guard Flesh:** your grafted arm can spend Blood to reduce the next limb-targeting damage.

## Warden telegraphs

The facilitator will read a visible cue before each action. Say what you think it means, then choose your response. No exact damage, order, or future action is shown here.
