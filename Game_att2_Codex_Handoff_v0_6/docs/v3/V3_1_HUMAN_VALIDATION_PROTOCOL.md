# V3-1 Human Validation Protocol

Status: READY; NO HUMAN RESULTS CLAIMED

## Primary claims requiring humans

1. Body capability vs Attention access is understood.
2. Weighted access feels constrained rather than arbitrary.
3. Shaded duty is understood as body insufficiency, not UI failure.
4. Source degradation feels causally connected to changed options.
5. Yellow/Red defense grammar is readable.
6. Player wants to change body/Brain strategy after failure.

## Session minimum

Use a fixed build/config and record:
- anonymous participant;
- build/commit;
- seed;
- Brain architecture;
- body state;
- each Attention refresh;
- facilitator intervention;
- errors/questions;
- debrief;
- voluntary replay choice.

## Non-leading debrief

- What determined which actions your character could physically perform?
- What determined which actions appeared right now?
- Why did this slot become unavailable?
- What changed when the limb was damaged?
- Which decision felt outside your control?
- What would you change in your body or Brain before another attempt?

## Pass/revise

PASS direction if participants can distinguish physical capability from cognitive access
and do not repeatedly interpret legal weighted variance as arbitrary denial.

REVISE if class-guaranteed duties routinely shade in normal builds, Focus/redraw become
mandatory taxes, or players cannot predict qualitative Brain/body tendencies.

No result may be recorded until actual participants run the fixture.
