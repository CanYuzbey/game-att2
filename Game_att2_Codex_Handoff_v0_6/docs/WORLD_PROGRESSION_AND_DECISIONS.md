Tool claims require the exact owned object and usable condition. Kit/cutter are not
automatically recovered from a killed actor: no additional item-loot route is
authorized. If a tool is handed over, later graft requiring it is illegal; Blood
progress remains possible. Service progress is authored before defeat, checked once,
preserved across retreat and not reset by closing the panel. Inspecting the optional
story pocket alone opens no passage and awards no gameplay power.

If Rusk/Mara surrenders, preserving life outranks the equipment/intel Goal. It offers
its legally transferable Right Arm with the P08 procedure or reward-free passage/
mercy. Ruined source or no surgical tool uses the visible reward-free concession.
This avoids forcing a kill when transfer cannot happen. The normal actor's resulting
departure/stand-aside shows the goal revision; it is not a generic loot container.

Rusk visibly inspects its empty arm socket and checks tools before interaction;
during combat it uses its intact clamp, protects it if a legal response exists,
and does not trade a ruined limb as Clean. Mara examines the lookout, declares
scouting demand, and targets the player's living vital under lethal stakes while
preserving its own escape Legs. Boneatour visibly guards the passage, points to its
horns when asserting status, then changes Charge after real horn loss. None needs
a new off-screen life simulation or institutional lore.
