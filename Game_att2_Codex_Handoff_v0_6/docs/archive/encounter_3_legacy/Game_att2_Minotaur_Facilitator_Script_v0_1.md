# Minotaur Warden Facilitator Script v0.1

> **SUPERSEDED BEFORE ANY VALID HUMAN SESSION.** Its fixed sequences, Bleeding, medical items, Table Loan, and four-round resolution conflict with the current decision. Use the bounded causal v0.2 specification and facilitator policy cards.

## Opening: read verbatim

"Thank you for helping test this paper game encounter. I am testing the system, not you. There is no correct strategy. Please think aloud when comfortable; you may also stay quiet. I will explain rules factually, but I cannot recommend an action."

"You will choose one table option, then play a short encounter. I will record choices, questions, and outcomes. We do not need your name or contact details in the record."

## Rules introduction: read verbatim

"Your body has a torso, a grafted right arm, and legs among other slots. Blood is both survival and a resource for actions. The table can integrate the arm, repair the torso, strengthen the legs, provide a loan, or leave the body unchanged."

"An Unstable graft can have an existing random result at the start of a round. Guard Flesh costs Blood and reduces the next limb-targeting damage. Bleeding costs Blood at round start; Clotting Cream removes one Bleeding effect. Blood Bag is a Fast item; Fast means it can be used before your normal action."

"A successful Knockdown can make you Downed. While Downed, you may use a Fast medical item, but your normal action must be spent to Stand. Standing does not also let you attack. Braced legs automatically prevent the first successful Knockdown in this encounter."

## Condition text

**Known only:** "Before the table, you hear distant hoof impacts, see a horned silhouette and a heavy cleaver, and find signs of charging force, torso danger, and balance pressure."

**Unknown only:** Do not read any condition text. Say: "Choose your table option now."

## During play

Read the exact telegraph from the encounter packet, then ask: "What do you think is about to happen?" Answer rules questions with the rules introduction only or the facilitator reference. Do not name the intended body preparation, reveal future actions, mention alternative strategies, or change a roll. Record help, invalid attempts, and participant interpretation.

## Closing: read verbatim

"Before I explain any design intent, I have a few questions about your experience." Ask the approved debrief questions in the Human Test Sheet. Do not explain what each Warden action was intended to test until all answers are recorded.

## Facilitator-only reference

- Script A: Charge, Cleave, Horn Hook, then Charge if no Downed; Horn Hook if Bleeding/torso-critical; otherwise Cleave.
- Script B: Horn Hook, Charge, Cleave, then Cleave if torso Damaged/Critical; otherwise Charge.
- Charge: d6 4-6 successful Knockdown, then 6 leg damage. Brace cancels first success.
- Cleave: 8 torso damage; Bleeding d6 5-6 normally or 4-6 if torso Damaged/Critical.
- Horn Hook: 8 right-arm damage; Guard Flesh may halve it.
- Resolve existing Unstable, Fast-item, collapse/soft-collapse, and debt rules exactly as documented. Settle Table Loan debt after round four. Never reveal this block to participants.
