## 4. Acceptance floor for a later authorized build

1. Move with real keyboard input, inspect the wounded initial body and pay finite
   Kit care without ticking while reading/caring/paused/alt-tabbed.
2. Enter contextual interaction in the same visible scene; no detached battle or
   range/reposition state.
3. Play a legal source/target card, reject an illegal source atomically, enforce
   one Preparation/Main, both Preparations before Mains, and one unstarted-Main
   reselection. Attention8/4/refill/redraw and Brain cost flags match P06/P09.
4. Yellow Block protects its declared target by weakening another legal guarding
   source; precise Parry prevents incoming contact and reduces visible Will;
   Red rejects Block/Parry and successful Evade adds no range state.
   Every accepted defense activation pays its declared1Blood even on success.
5. Across attempts, kill each ordinary NPC and collect bowl-first Blood once with
   no limb; accept legal living surrender and perform one exact graft with no
   kill-Blood. Inspect donor/recipient costs, wounds, quality, original-arm storage
   and negative dead/ruined/unauthorized/unaffordable/duplicate cases.
6. In Mara's fight, observe Grip gained after compatible graft plus deliberate
   study/swap; occupied clamp cannot attack/Block/Parry and cannot stop Charge.
   Verify return/human-arm replacement/source-ruin incompatibility separately.
7. Player Broken Will uses one pre-disclosed primary/counteroffer/Defy window;
   retreat6Blood happens once per ordinary encounter without resource/claim reset.
8. Both graft and no-graft witness paths reach Boneatour with legal body sources;
   Full normal arm handles Charge Parry, Strained does not. Evade avoids its contact/
   stun but does not stop it. Block never answers Charge.
9. Damage separate horns, observe18->12->noCharge, revalidate legal normal fallbacks,
   consume one Will-break Charge cancellation, and observe nonstacking next-round
   stun/recovery with defense and Torso rescue preserved.
10. Reach actual player death through paid depletion or real lethal body damage,
    never a hidden scripted override. Boss Blood/Integrity decrease normally with
    costs/damage and retain no regrowth/immortality. No free full-state resolved
    combat cycle exists; paused indefinite deliberation remains ordinary pause.
11. Retry restores initial world/body/deck/uses with another authored person and no
    previous-person memories; session Concept/Brain options remain while app open,
    no Memory/death reward, and only settings survive cold launch/crash.
12. Continue play after care/kill/graft/claim/retreat until terminal/retry and reproduce
    critical mutations with versioned logs, negative tests and actual input smoke.
