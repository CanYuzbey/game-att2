# Game att2 — Paper-Test Evidence v0.1

## Evidence classification

All tests so far are designer-run internal paper simulations, sometimes with Can making player choices. They are useful for contradiction/exploit discovery. They are not blind playtests and do not establish market demand, accessibility, emotional impact, or fun across players.

## T0 — Jeff baseline

Observed:

- desired right arm created immediate motivation;
- Claim → damage → Hell Saw → harvest created a strong identity moment;
- choosing arms over 25 blood made body progression compete with safety;
- emergency graft completed the fantasy.

Revisions triggered:

- thresholds;
- action-limb impairment;
- harvest quality;
- tool-use rules;
- limb-loss surrender pressure.

## T1 — no-spend exploit

Observed:

- free Grip Strike spam could disable both arms while preserving blood;
- if free damage yielded good parts, blood economy failed.

Control added:

- basic damage cannot create Clean Harvest;
- incapacity can win/surrender, but parts remain ruined/low value;
- salvage costs blood and risk.

## T2 — failed Hell Saw/death spiral

Observed:

- bleeding + expensive failure + Rage + medical cost could collapse rapidly;
- danger was appropriate, but warning/counterplay needed clarity.

Controls added:

- structured transaction/log warnings;
- Fast medical timing;
- Panic Pulse clarification;
- one tutorial soft-loss/low-blood valve.

## T3 — Anna medical

Observed:

- Fast Blood Bag/Clotting Cream let medical choices coexist with offense;
- Anna felt distinct from Jeff;
- stabilization could contest limb greed;
- Stabilized penalty must be visible.

Controls added:

- Fast tag;
- Stabilized resolution;
- Marked salvage discount;
- tuned Anna Bleeding trigger.

## T4 — Bone-Minotaur stress test

Observed:

- old Focus-as-main-action was a trap;
- heavy threat was too lethal for current post-Jeff state;
- leg/torso counterplay needed further design.

Decision:

- Focus became pre-action;
- Bone-Minotaur delayed beyond simulator v0.1.

## T5 — Many-Eyed Flesh complexity test

Observed:

- abnormal body, hidden core, countering, restrain, reduced gains, unstable graft, and exposure windows overloaded the base test.

Decision:

- delay and simplify later.

## T6 — starting body variants

Observed:

- S-001 best tutorial because missing right arm creates desire with manageable complexity;
- S-002 better as Core-target pressure later;
- S-003 too noisy for tutorial;
- S-004 debt start should be challenge/unlock.

## Forced Unstable test

Observed:

- automatic disable was harsh;
- choice-oriented Twitch and positive Surge made volatility more acceptable;
- backup action and near-term stabilization are necessary.

## Mini-campaign v0.4

Sequence:

- S-001 at 85 blood;
- acquire Jeff arm;
- emergency graft Unstable;
- Anna tests graft and offers stabilization;
- accept treatment rather than more limb greed;
- integrate arm at table;
- finish at 37 blood with changed body and remaining damaged torso.

Reconciliation note: 37 Blood is retained as historical paper evidence only. Current authoritative simulation resolves seed 42 to 25 Blood under Combat Rules v0.4/configuration. The missing configured spare-arm sale and paper arithmetic make the records unreconciled; 37 is not an automated expected value and 25 is not an intended balance target.

Gate result:

- pass for deterministic Python simulator;
- not pass for Unity;
- simulator must now test repeated numerical behavior and contradictions.
