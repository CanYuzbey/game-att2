## Coding guide

Telegraph: `understood immediately`, `understood after rules clarification`, `misinterpreted but adapted`, `never understood`.

Reasoning: `threat-specific preparation`, `general survival preparation`, `Blood conservation`, `debt-enabled preparation`, `aesthetic/intuitive choice`, `random/guess`, `rule misunderstanding`.

Regret: `no regret`, `productive regret`, `unfair-feeling regret`, `rule-confusion regret`.

Stand: `meaningful tactical cost`, `acceptable inconvenience`, `repetitive frustration`, `rule confusion`.

Guard: `recognized and used`, `recognized and declined`, `missed despite sufficient information`, `unavailable due to state`, `blocked by Unstable outcome`.

Examples are labels only, not predicted participant results.
