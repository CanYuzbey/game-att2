# Game att2 Encounter 3 Participant Cards v0.2

Do not show facilitator policies. Before the table, show the Shared body/table card and only the assigned Known or Unknown block. Show the Action/target card when the encounter begins; this prevents the Unknown condition from seeing the capability model before its table choice.

## Shared body and table card

You begin before the table with 40 Blood: Intact head, Damaged torso without Bleeding, Intact left arm, an Intact Stabilized Grafted right arm that is not Integrated, Weak legs, and Intact core. Panic Pulse and Brace are unused. Hell Saw and Bone Scissors are available once; Claim the Cut, Blood Bag, and Clotting Cream are unavailable.

| Choice | Starting Blood | Visible effect |
|---|---:|---|
| Integrate Grafted Arm | 25 | Graft becomes Integrated; Guard Flesh remains available |
| Repair Damaged Torso | 22 | Torso becomes Intact |
| Strengthen Weak Legs | 28 | Gain Braced legs and Anchored: automatically prevent the first valid Knockdown attempt without consuming Brace |
| Leave Unchanged | 40 | Body does not change |

Brace remains available once as a main action and prevents one additional Knockdown attempt during its protected round. Guard Flesh uses its existing Blood cost and protects against the next limb-targeting damage; it does not prevent Knockdown.

## Action and target card

- One main action is available. Focus occurs before it and reveals the current exact Warden source and target; it does not consume the main action.
- If Knocked Down, Focus remains legal, but the next main action must be Stand. Stand cannot also attack, use a tool, Guard, or Brace.
- You may target Warden right arm, legs, or torso. Damaging a source can weaken its actions; making it unusable removes them.
- Grip Strike deals normal damage but cannot produce Clean Harvest.
- Hell Saw follows its existing cost and roll; right arm and legs are Large. A sever produces no harvest or reward.
- Bone Scissors is visible but has no valid target because every represented Warden zone is too large.

## Known Threat card

> The next opponent creates heavy impact and Knockdown pressure. Its major attacks depend on its legs and weapon-bearing arm. Damaging an action source can weaken or remove the actions that depend on it.

Capability summary: legs source Charge and Hoof Follow-up; right arm sources Butcher Strike; torso is a structural incapacity target. Exact current intent remains visible during combat.

## Unknown Threat card

> A physically powerful opponent is waiting. You do not know exactly which part of your current body it will test.

Do not show the capability summary until the encounter begins. Visible current intent is shown normally during combat.

## Ending card

The test ends if your Blood reaches 0 and you die, the Warden becomes
combat-incapacitated under the represented target rules, or eight rounds complete.
Warden incapacity is not death. Limb for Life is unavailable in this fixture. This
test has no Warden loot, surrender, escape, bargain, or harvest.
